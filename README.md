Collection of Assignments for CSE 3000: Contemporary Issues in Computer Science and Engineering

My take on AI ethics agrees with chapter 5. Companies/services promote values which sound wonderful but rarely follow them themselves. We do need to emphasise more concrete guidelines, which everyone should abide by. Consumers should be prioritised, and embedding ethics into everyday use of AI can help improve consumer trust. Normalising ethical concerns and assigning clear responsibilties to departments to monitor activity and ensure concerns are resolved. To conclude, simply being truthful about simple privacy terms and monitoring agents so that ethical standards are held would help immensely.
